# Ledger launch film — creative direction

## Intent

A 51-second standalone launch film for Ledger. The film turns one ticker into a visible chain of
evidence, calculation, judgment, and conversation. It uses the live NVIDIA report generated on
2026-10-21; no placeholder company data appears in the cut.

## Reference grammar

- **Rebuilt for Collectors:** stark black/white grounds, short declarative copy, and generous holds.
- **We're Building the Annotated Internet:** one visual thread carries the viewer between product
  ideas instead of treating each scene as a separate slide.
- **Agentic Payments — Match Day:** the conversation becomes the interface payoff.

The film borrows pacing and structural ideas only. It contains no frames, audio, artwork, or copy
from the references.

## Storyboard

| Time | Movement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:03.5 | “A ticker is not a thesis.” | Establish the problem in one sentence. |
| 00:03.5–00:08 | NVDA is typed into the Ledger search | Show the real starting action. |
| 00:08–00:13.5 | Filing, Company Facts, and price records enter | Make the evidence chain visible. |
| 00:13.5–00:19 | Exact metrics count into place | Separate deterministic math from language. |
| 00:19–00:31 | The memo scrolls through all five sections | Deliver the product’s money shot and show the report’s depth. |
| 00:31–00:44 | Report answers “why?”, then Explore checks public context | Make both conversation modes the product payoff. |
| 00:44–00:47.5 | Calculation, citation, and boundary checks | State the trust model without a security lecture. |
| 00:47.5–00:51 | Ledger lockup | Close on the product promise. |

## Production rules

- 30 fps; native 16:9 and 9:16 compositions from one scene tree.
- Every number shown comes from the live NVDA report visible in the application.
- Motion is frame-driven and deterministic. No generated footage is required.
- The original ambient score is generated locally by `scripts/generate-audio.py`. Warm pads,
  felt-key tones, and soft harmonic blooms replace rhythmic impacts and carry no third-party rights.
- The product remains a research aid; the closing frame states that it is not investment advice.
